About Our Clinic

Providing Quality Clinical Care for a Healthier Tomorrow

Founded in 2012, CarePlus Clinic has grown into a leading community healthcare facility. We believe that medical care should be compassionate, highly accessible, and personalized. Our modern space is designed to put you at ease, and our professional medical staff utilizes advanced diagnostics to deliver reliable clinical outcomes.

Our Core Commitments

  • Patient-Centered Focus: We treat the person, not just the symptoms. Your voice and comfort are our top priorities.
  • Integrity & Care Quality: We provide transparent diagnoses and treatment options rooted in evidence-based medicine.
  • Continuous Innovation: Regularly upgrading our equipment and clinical protocols to match the latest standards.
Our Specializations

Comprehensive Medical Services

CarePlus Clinic offers a wide array of outpatient clinical services to support your health journey at every life stage.

General Medicine

Comprehensive primary care, physical exams, preventative health advice, and chronic disease management for all family members.

Cardiology

Advanced cardiovascular health assessments, ECG screenings, blood pressure management, and heart condition diagnostics.

Pediatrics

Gentle and expert child healthcare, immunization programs, developmental milestones tracking, and pediatric checkups.

Dermatology

Expert evaluation of skin, hair, and nail health. Treatment for acne, eczema, mole mapping, and diagnostic skin biopsies.

Dental Care

Preventative cleanings, routine checkups, root canal procedures, fillings, and cosmetic dentistry treatments for a bright smile.

Diagnostics & Lab

On-site blood panels, rapid urinalysis, digital X-rays, ultra-fast lab reporting, and accurate diagnostic screening services.
